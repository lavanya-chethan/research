# Reddit Marketing Research for B2B SaaS

## Overview

This repository contains structured research on **Reddit marketing as a B2B SaaS growth channel**.

The goal of this research is to understand how startups and SaaS companies use Reddit for:

- Organic customer acquisition
- Community-led growth
- Brand trust building
- AI search visibility
- Social listening
- Audience research
- Founder-led distribution
- Product feedback loops

Reddit is evolving beyond a discussion platform and becoming an important channel for:

- Google search visibility
- ChatGPT / AI citations
- Buyer trust validation
- Long-tail demand capture

This research collects practical insights directly from experts actively working in Reddit growth, community marketing, and SaaS demand generation.

---

## Research Objective

The purpose of this repository is to build a high-signal knowledge base that can later be used to create a practical Reddit growth playbook for B2B SaaS companies.

The focus areas include:

- Subreddit discovery
- Community participation
- Founder branding
- Reddit Ads
- AMA strategies
- Community moderation
- Content distribution
- AI visibility through Reddit mentions
- Reputation management

## Data Collection Methods

### LinkedIn

Method used:

- Manual collection
- Activity feed review
- Post URL extraction
- Summary creation

Why:
LinkedIn has strong anti-scraping restrictions, so manual collection provides higher-quality structured data.

---

### YouTube

Method used:

- `youtube-transcript-api`

Process:

1. Extract video ID
2. Run transcript script
3. Store transcript in markdown

Why:
Fastest and most reliable way to capture expert long-form content.

## Key Research Themes Identified

Across all experts, recurring themes emerged:

### 1. Trust before promotion

Most Reddit experts strongly emphasize:

**Contribute first. Promote later.**

---

### 2. Reddit impacts AI visibility

Reddit conversations are increasingly cited in:

- ChatGPT
- Perplexity
- Google AI Overviews

---

### 3. Community-led growth compounds

Long-term participation creates:

- Brand trust
- Loyal audiences
- Better conversion quality

---

### 4. Founder branding matters

Founder visibility in Reddit communities often performs better than corporate promotion.

---

### 5. Social listening is underutilized

Reddit is one of the strongest platforms for:

- Customer pain points
- Competitor insights
- Feature feedback
- Market sentiment

## What I Learned

This research helped me understand that Reddit marketing is less about "posting" and more about:

- Community trust
- Audience psychology
- Strategic contribution
- Visibility through helpfulness

It also highlighted how Reddit is becoming a major influence layer in both search and AI discovery.
